Communication job: By the end, a product, sponsor, or demo audience should understand how WebAccessible turns a plain-language errand into a visible autonomous browser run, why the experience is approachable for older adults, where human control remains, and how the caregiver/memory/cloud layers support trust.

Narrative arc
1. Introduce the product with the actual landing experience.
2. Establish why conventional websites fail the intended user.
3. Show the participant’s passwordless task launcher.
4. Explain the visible agent run in three stages.
5. Make the surviving safety boundaries explicit.
6. Show the caregiver console and cloud evidence.
7. Explain grounded recall and proactive reminders.
8. Map the sponsor stack to product responsibilities.
9. Close on the product promise.

Visual approach: Codex Grid’s restrained white canvas, strong black typography, and thin rules, with WebAccessible forest green as the single accent. Current application screenshots are the primary evidence.
