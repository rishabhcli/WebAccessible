# Track 2: Value of Intelligence Service Definition

## Service

**WebAccessible Care Plan** is a $15/month caregiver subscription for one older adult. It gives the family a private library of recurring online routines, one-step browser guidance when the adult is observably stuck, deterministic low-cost replay, a completion history, and escalation to the caregiver when risk or ambiguity is high.

The payer is Susan, not Margaret. The outcome Susan buys is fewer live troubleshooting calls while Margaret keeps agency for recurring online tasks.

## Customer, job, and willingness to pay

| Element | Definition |
|---|---|
| Paying user | Adult child/caregiver supporting one older adult remotely. |
| User | Older adult completing 5-6 recurring browser tasks. |
| Core job | Help the adult finish familiar tasks without taking over the browser. |
| Pain removed | Repeated “I can't find the thing” calls, uncertainty about completed bills/tasks, and unsafe unfamiliar requests. |
| Package | Up to six saved routines, one supported adult, caregiver session history, and escalation notifications. |
| Price | $15/month per supported adult ($180 ARR). |
| Unit proof | One real paid/prepaid pilot family, two runs of one task, and a documented change in the caregiver's support burden. |

The $15/month plan creates a clear path above Track 2's $10 ARR-per-user threshold. It is a pricing hypothesis until a payment is captured; no doc, demo, or submission copy may call it revenue beforehand.

## What makes the intelligence valuable

- **Cold-run intelligence:** Browserbase hosts the task browser; bounded guidance turns observable stuck signals into one verified next step.
- **Memory compounding:** EverOS persists the teach run as a readable skill and the outcome as an episode.
- **Cost collapse:** deterministic selector-first replay removes general website reasoning on stable steps.
- **Accountability:** Snowflake records every step, model cost, replay state, and outcome so the family sees measurable service value.

## Pilot conversion flow

1. Susan completes a five-minute setup and selects one recurring task.
2. She completes one teach run with Margaret in the Browserbase Live View session.
3. WebAccessible shows the readable EverOS skill and the real Snowflake cost record.
4. Margaret completes the same task through warm replay while retaining every click.
5. Susan starts a paid/prepaid Care Plan after seeing that repeatable support is working.
